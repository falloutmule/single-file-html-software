# Built-in asset notice

The original paper-cut backgrounds and starter stickers are procedural SVG artwork authored for The Imaginarium. They are generated entirely from project source in `src/model/builtInLibrary.js` and do not contain third-party characters, logos, or downloaded art.

The separately identified Blockfolk category and Blockfolk Valley background are user-supplied raster artwork. Their provenance and redistribution authorization are recorded in `BLOCKFOLK-ASSETS.md`.

Emoji stickers are actual Unicode emoji characters rendered by the device's installed color-emoji font. The project does not embed, copy, download, or claim ownership of an emoji font or vendor-specific emoji artwork; appearance can differ by operating system.
