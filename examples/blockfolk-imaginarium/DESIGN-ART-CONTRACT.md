# BlockFolk Imaginarium World Art Contract

## Approved production background

BlockFolk Valley is one square, full-bleed 4096 × 4096 world mapped directly to the product's 4096 × 4096 logical coordinates. The visual source is the user-supplied and user-approved JPEG retained at `art/source/blockfolk-valley-authority-1280.jpg`; this record makes no licensing, generation-provider, or public-redistribution claim.

The production master is a deterministic NVIDIA RTX Video Super Resolution upscale of that exact source. Its reproducible workflow is retained under `art/workflows/`, and the browser uses one optimized 4096 × 4096 WebP under `src/assets/backgrounds/`. Replacing that single shipping asset must not require changes to camera, sticker, save, puzzle, or export architecture.

## Composition invariants

- Preserve the square full-bleed composition, diagonal ocean coastline and beach, mountains and snow, mountain lake and waterfall, complete river, central plains, forest extent, clearings, trails and junctions, terrain elevations, vegetation, rocks, camera angle, lighting, colors, voxel scale, and softened painterly voxel treatment.
- The approved narrow natural terrain trails, including mountain, forest, and lower trails, are part of the production map and are explicitly allowed.
- Preserve useful open placement areas on mountain ledges, forest clearings, plains, coast, and riverbanks.

## Geography

- Upper-left: ocean, diagonal coast, beach, rocky shore, and river mouth.
- Upper-right: rocky mountains, limited snow, lake, waterfall, and natural headwaters.
- Lower-right: mixed forest, clearings, river corridor, and natural trails.
- Lower-left and center: broad plains, meadow, calm river bend, and natural trails.
- Continuous watercourse: mountain lake/source → waterfall and rocky descent → forest channel → plains bend → ocean mouth.

## Forbidden additions

Do not add buildings, bridges, docks, fences, signs, constructed roads, constructed paths, ruins, portals, statues, furniture, characters, animals, creatures, or magic objects to the permanent background. Do not redraw, regenerate, inpaint, reinterpret, crop, or alter the approved map.

Sticker production and later interface illustration remain separate phases and are not authorized by this contract.
