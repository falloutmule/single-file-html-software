# BlockFolk Imaginarium World Art Contract

This document defines the replacement contract for the engineering-only `blockfolk-valley` world asset. It does not authorize production art in the world-foundation build.

## Production background

- One square, full-bleed terrain image using the existing 4096 × 4096 logical world.
- Bright late-spring daylight in a handcrafted, softened voxel-diorama style.
- Terrain only: no permanent structures, characters, animals, creatures, or magic objects.
- Replace the single `blockfolk-valley` debug asset without changing camera, sticker, save, or export architecture.

## Geography

- Top-left: diagonal ocean bay, broad river mouth, beach, rocky coast, and grassy shore.
- Top-right: mixed rocky mountains, limited snow, green foothills, and natural headwaters.
- Bottom-right: mixed pine and broadleaf forest, clearings, and a river corridor.
- Bottom-left: broad rolling plains, meadow, and a wide calm river.
- Continuous river: mountain pool/source → rocky descent → forest channel → plains bend → ocean mouth.
- Keep useful open placement areas on mountain ledges, forest clearings, plains, coast, and riverbanks.

## Forbidden permanent content

Buildings, bridges, docks, fences, signs, constructed roads or paths, ruins, portals, statues, furniture, characters, animals, and creatures are forbidden in the background.

## Current engineering asset

The shipped pre-art world is a deterministic code-generated SVG marked `DEBUG WORLD • NOT PRODUCTION`. Its colors, labels, geometry, typography, and linework are camera-test aids and are not production design decisions.
