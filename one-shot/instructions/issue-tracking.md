# Decisions and issues

Keep `DECISIONS.md` and `ISSUES-ENCOUNTERED.md` current during work. Each issue records ID, phase, status, severity, observed and expected behavior, evidence, best explanation, attempts, resolution/workaround, affected files, verification, and remaining risk.

Use `OPEN`, `INVESTIGATING`, `RESOLVED`, `WORKAROUND`, `DEFERRED`, `UNVERIFIED`, `BLOCKED`, or `SUPERSEDED` for issue state. Do not delete failed evidence; explain why a later conclusion supersedes it.
